## Dataset contract required by the implemented evaluator

The evaluator consumes chronological, completed 5m or 15m bars (`ReplayBar`) and caller-supplied point-in-time inputs. It does not compute indicators or establish a historical universe. Minimum usable package:

- **Cash series per eligible underlying:** timestamp with `+05:30` exchange-session interpretation, open/high/low/close/volume, instrument ID, symbol/series, exchange, completeness/quality flags. At least 12 months is the documented initial target; obtain minute or finer source data if rebuilding 5m bars/indicators, or validated 5m bars otherwise.
- **Futures OI:** timestamped OHLCV and OI for the specific stock-futures contract, contract ID, expiry, underlying ID and mapping validity dates. Preserve individual expiries; no undocumented continuous-series splice. Minimum history must span the same evaluation window and the 20-session indicator warm-up.
- **Score inputs aligned as of each bar close:** ATR, RVOL, day return, daily sigma20, OI daily change, VWAP/EMA20 side, and any supplied sector classification. Missing values stay missing. Structure and zones come from replay. Q-019 is still open; do not synthesize sector direction. A provider must document whether each field is raw, adjusted, delayed, or derived and its calculation timestamp/version.
- **Point-in-time universe and identity:** dated F&O eligible constituents including additions/removals, suspended/delisted symbols, cash and futures instrument IDs/tokens, series, underlying/expiry/lot-size mappings, and effective-from/to dates. A current instrument master alone cannot control survivorship bias.
- **Sessions and corporate actions:** official dated trading calendar including holidays, special/Muhurat sessions, session opens/closes, and early closes. NSE publishes [market timings/holiday information](https://www.nseindia.com/resources/exchange-communication-holidays), [contract information](https://www.nseindia.com/static/products-services/equity-derivatives-contract-information), and [F&O corporate-action adjustment rules](https://www.nseindia.com/static/products-services/equity-derivatives-corporate-actions-adjustments); confirm whether the selected provider supplies a complete historical archive usable for this dataset. Provide timestamped corporate-action events and adjustment factors (at least splits, bonuses, rights, mergers/name or symbol changes) or a documented raw/adjusted series policy. Reconcile adjusted price history with the raw execution-like OHLC used by replay; do not silently mix them. Dividends/total-return treatment is not implemented and must be excluded or separately documented.
- **Provenance and rights metadata:** legal source, source dataset/reference, as-of/retrieval date, correction/revision policy, permitted research purpose, storage/retention and deletion terms, and explicit rights for internal historical evaluation. If future display is contemplated, obtain separate rights for product display, end-user counts, derived scores/indicators, screenshots/snapshots, caching, redistribution, geography, channels and retention.

The pipeline's permission field is only an owner attestation; it cannot assess contract scope. The current evaluator reports gaps and missing OI but lacks indicator recalculation, a true point-in-time universe builder, corporate-action adjustment, benchmark/baseline comparisons, and strategy-performance validation.

### What is verified versus still unknown

- **Verified from official public pages:** NSE's agreement-based licensing framework and current published domestic tariffs for specifically named products; TrueData's stated API fields, 6-month intraday API history and separate redistribution approval requirement; GFDL's published sub-12-month intraday backfill windows and quote-based API pricing; Dhan's documented five-year intraday endpoint for active instruments; Kite's documented OHLCV/OI endpoint and public-data restrictions; Upstox V3's documented one-minute OHLCV/OI response example, Jan 2022 minute-history statement, and one-month query cap for 1–15 minute bars.
- **Published prices, not ZoneScan quotes:** NSE's listed tariff items only, with the stated effective date, site/medium basis and applicable exclusions. These are not a quote for the required full bundle. TrueData consumer terminal/Velocity prices are not API or product redistribution prices. No GFDL or Dhan commercial product quote was found.
- **Unverified:** coverage of historical (including removed/delisted) F&O names and contract IDs; exact 12-month or longer 1m/5m cash + futures OI availability; correction/adjustment methodology; complete corporate-action/session archive; full universe membership history; permitted research storage duration; derived score/indicator treatment; user/site/channel thresholds; product-specific approvals, fees, taxes, minimum commitment, SLA and termination/export rights. For Upstox, the public V3 page's timestamp example/table type discrepancy also needs provider confirmation if numeric timestamps are encountered.

## Minimum dataset acceptance criteria

Do not load data into the evaluation pipeline until **all** applicable criteria pass:

- Executed license or written authorization explicitly covers internal historical research, fields, history window, storage, derived analytics, permitted personnel, retention and audit.
- Received dataset manifest contains source/product/version, license reference, retrieval/as-of date, coverage bounds, timezone, bar convention, adjustment/correction policy, field definitions, and checksums or another stable integrity record.
- At least 12 months of aligned 5m cash bars (or sufficient 1m data to derive them consistently), 20 prior completed sessions for time-of-day volume baseline, and same-window futures OI plus daily history needed for sigma/ATR. No mandatory field may be silently zero-filled.
- Per-bar cash OHLCV and futures contract OI retain timestamp, source instrument/contract IDs and expiry; mappings are time-valid and rollovers remain explicit.
- Point-in-time F&O universe is supplied for every session, including entries/exits and unavailable/delisted constituents, or the evaluation explicitly declares survivorship control unavailable and is not used for Gate 2 conclusions.
- Calendar and corporate-action records cover the evaluation window; session gaps, special sessions and action adjustments reconcile under a written rule. Unresolved actions are flagged/excluded with disclosed counts, not silently adjusted.
- Validator reports no unexplained duplicates, ordering, OHLC, timestamp, mapping, or provenance errors. Missing intervals, missing OI/indicators and excluded instruments are quantified before replay.
- A second reviewer signs off the dataset manifest, contract interpretation, evaluator version/parameters, train/validation/test chronology and leakage/survivorship controls.
